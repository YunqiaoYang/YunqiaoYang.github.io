Yunqiao Yang (杨昀樵)

I am a 1st year Ph.D. student at the Multimedia Laboratory (MMLab), The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), advised by Prof. Hongsheng Li.

I received my B.E. in Electronic Science and Technology from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2025.

My research interests lie in Large Language Models (LLMs), specifically focusing on Preference Optimization, Mathematical Reasoning, and Code Generation Agents.

💻 Projects & Resources

We open-source the code and datasets for our research to facilitate reproducibility and further exploration.

  • SlidesGen-Bench
    A benchmark for evaluating AI-generated presentations across Content Fidelity, Visual Aesthetics, and Editability, together with the Slides-Align1.5k human preference dataset. [Homepage]
  • WebGen-Bench
    The first benchmark for evaluating LLM-based agents on generating websites from scratch. [Dataset]
  • PCPO: Preference Optimization Framework
    A novel framework that establishes dual quantitative metrics (surface-level correctness & token-level consistency) for preference selection in LLMs.
